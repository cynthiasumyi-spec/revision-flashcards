Look around the school fair

The case

Leo was looking after the raffle box at the school charity fair. The box is gone, and Leo has not come back.

How to play

  1. Tap a place on the map.
  2. Talk to a person, or search.
  3. Clues are written in the notebook.
  4. When you have all the notes, tap Solve Case and say who took the box, and why.
