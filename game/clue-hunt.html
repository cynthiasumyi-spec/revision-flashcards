The case

Leo was guarding the raffle box at the school charity fair. The box is gone, and Leo has not come back.

How to play

  1. Tap a place on the map.
  2. Talk to a person, or search.
  3. Clues are written in the notebook.
  4. When the notes are ready, tap Solve Case and name who took the box, and why.
